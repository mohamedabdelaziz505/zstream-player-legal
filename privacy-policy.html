Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZStream Player ("the app") handles information when you use the Android mobile or Android TV application.

Information stored by the app

The app stores information locally on your device to provide its features, including IPTV source names and endpoints, source credentials you enter, favorites, playback and download state, selected folders, and app preferences. Source credentials are stored using the platform's secure storage facilities where supported. This information is not uploaded to our own server by the app.

Information sent to services you choose

When you add an IPTV, M3U, Xtream, or EPG source, the app connects to the endpoint you provide to validate the source and load channels, movies, series, and programme data. When you play or download media, the app requests that media from the provider's server. That provider may receive your IP address, request details, and account credentials according to its own privacy policy. Only add sources and media you are authorized to use.

Advertising

The mobile version may display banner advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction information, and diagnostics to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to Google's policies and your device or regional privacy choices. Android TV does not display the app's banner ad.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics and logs

The app may keep local diagnostic logs to help troubleshoot playback, downloads, and source loading. Logs are intended to remain on the device unless you explicitly share them. Do not share logs that contain source URLs or credentials.

Permissions

The app may request network access, notifications, foreground-service access for downloads, and user-selected file or folder access for saving downloads. Permissions are used only for the related feature and can be managed in Android settings.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored app data remains until you remove a source, delete downloads, clear app data, or uninstall the app. To request help with privacy questions, contact us using the email below.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page. For privacy questions, contact m.abdelaziz9009@gmail.com.

يوضح هذا المستند طريقة تعامل تطبيق ZStream Player مع البيانات عند استخدامه على الهاتف أو Android TV.

البيانات المحفوظة على الجهاز

يحفظ التطبيق محليًا بيانات المصادر التي تدخلها، مثل الاسم والرابط وبيانات Xtream، بالإضافة إلى المفضلة وحالة المشاهدة والتنزيلات والمجلدات والإعدادات. تُحفظ بيانات الدخول باستخدام التخزين الآمن في النظام حيثما كان ذلك متاحًا، ولا يرفع التطبيق هذه البيانات إلى خادم تابع لنا.

المصادر التي تختارها

يتصل التطبيق بخوادم IPTV أو M3U أو Xtream أو EPG التي تختارها لتحميل البيانات وتشغيل أو تنزيل الوسائط. قد يرى مزود الخدمة عنوان IP وبيانات الطلب وبيانات الدخول وفقًا لسياسة الخصوصية الخاصة به. أضف فقط مصادر ووسائط تملك حق استخدامها.

الإعلانات

قد يعرض إصدار الهاتف إعلانات بانر من Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). قد تعالج Google وشركاؤها معرّفات الجهاز وعنوان IP ومعلومات التفاعل والتشخيص لأغراض الإعلانات وفقًا لسياساتها وخيارات الخصوصية في منطقتك. لا يعرض إصدار Android TV إعلان البانر الخاص بالتطبيق.
